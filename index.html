Quick Answers

Why is my deposit not showing in game balance?
Deposits usually reflect within 5 to 15 minutes. If it is delayed due to bank server issues, please submit your UTR / Transaction ID in the Deposit Problem section for quick resolution.
How long does withdrawal take?
Standard withdrawals are processed within 15 minutes to 2 hours depending on your selected payment method (UPI, Bank Transfer, or E-Wallet).
Is my password safe here?
Your account security is important. Never share your password with anyone, and ensure your connection is secure before submitting any support requests.
